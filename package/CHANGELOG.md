**1.0.0**

First release. Key steering for every saddled mount (the Odin Horse, the Lox, Mount Up's wolf, boar and anything else it saddles, a bear included): W walks up to a trot, Shift gallops, letting go stops, and A and D turn it with its own turning legs, in place too. Each animal keeps its own pace: the Odin Horse builds up gently to a trot, a wolf or boar is quick off the mark, and W is at least a trot on every mount. A ridden wolf leaps with its legs stretched out like in its run, instead of springing up stiff-legged. Every mount moves smoothly under you (Mount Up's wolf used to move in visible jerks). The camera turns with your mount. Alt + E anywhere on a tamed, saddled animal gets you on, for big animals whose body hides the saddle. J whistles for the tame you last rode and it comes to you, and a mount you get off stays where you left it. While a bow is drawn from the saddle the camera stops at the edge of the Odin Horse's bow arc, so arrows fly along the crosshair. Attacking from the saddle no longer rolls the camera. A ridden Odin Horse no longer rears to attack on its own. With Better Archery, the rider no longer flickers while drawing. The mount panel shows the animal you ride instead of a Lox. These riding fixes moved here from PotatoTweaks.
